# 🏥 TechCare

### Bringing Healthcare Closer to You

TechCare is a patient-centered healthcare platform designed to make healthcare services more accessible, especially for elderly people, people with disabilities, patients with mobility difficulties, and anyone who may struggle to reach traditional healthcare services.

The platform connects **patients, doctors, nurses, pharmacies, and blood donors** in one unified healthcare ecosystem.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Healthcare can become difficult when the patient is unable to move easily.

Finding a doctor, arranging a home nurse, searching for available medicine, or finding a compatible blood donor can require multiple disconnected services.

**TechCare aims to solve this problem by bringing these services together in a single platform.**

The patient can discover healthcare providers based on **location, availability, rating, and price**, request services from home, manage prescriptions and medicines, and submit blood requests when needed.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Many people face difficulties accessing healthcare because of:

* Physical disabilities or mobility limitations
* Old age
* Chronic illnesses
* Injuries or recovery periods
* Difficulty traveling to clinics or hospitals
* Difficulty finding nearby healthcare providers
* Difficulty locating available medicines
* Difficulty finding compatible blood donors

TechCare is designed to reduce these barriers.

# 🩸 Blood Donation

TechCare also provides a blood donation request system.

A patient or authorized requester can submit:

* Required blood type
* Required quantity
* Location
* Hospital / treatment location
* Urgency
* Request details

The platform can identify nearby compatible donors and notify them.

### Blood Request Flow

```text
Create Blood Request
        ↓
Blood Type + Location
        ↓
Find Compatible Donors
        ↓
Notify Nearby Donors
        ↓
Donor Accepts
        ↓
Donation Coordination
        ↓
Donation Completed
```

The platform can also provide a **points and recognition system** to encourage voluntary blood donation, subject to applicable medical, ethical, and legal requirements.

# 💰 Distance-Based Pricing

For home services, providers can define a base price and an additional price based on distance.

Example:

```text
Base Service Price: 500 EGP

Distance: 20 KM
Distance Rate: 5 EGP / KM

Distance Fee: 100 EGP

Final Price: 600 EGP
```

The patient can see the pricing breakdown before confirming the service.

---

# ⏱️ Booking Requests

TechCare can use a limited response period for service requests.

Example:

```text
Patient sends request
        ↓
Provider receives notification
        ↓
Provider has limited time to respond
        ↓
Accept → Booking Confirmed
Reject → Request Rejected
No Response → Request Expired
```

This helps prevent patients from waiting too long for an unavailable provider.
