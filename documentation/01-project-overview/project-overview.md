# 🩺 TechCare — Project Overview

> **TechCare is a web-based healthcare services platform designed to make healthcare more accessible, convenient, organized, secure, and location-aware by connecting patients with healthcare providers and essential medical services through one centralized platform.**

---

## 1. Introduction

TechCare is a centralized web-based healthcare platform that aims to simplify the process of finding, accessing, and managing healthcare services.

The platform brings multiple healthcare roles and services together in one digital environment instead of requiring patients to rely on separate platforms, phone calls, manual searches, or disconnected services.

TechCare is designed around the idea that healthcare should be easier to discover and easier to access, especially for users who may have difficulty traveling to traditional healthcare facilities.

The platform connects patients with:

- Doctors
- Nurses
- Pharmacies / Pharmacists
- Medical Laboratories
- Blood Donors

while providing administrators with tools to manage, monitor, and control the platform.

The platform also provides shared capabilities such as:

- Authentication
- Authorization
- User profiles
- Location services
- Search and discovery
- Booking and service requests
- Notifications
- Payments and financial tracking
- Ratings and reviews
- Complaints
- Provider verification
- Healthcare information management

---

# 2. Project Idea

The core idea of TechCare is to create a **single digital healthcare ecosystem** where patients can find and request different healthcare services according to their needs and location.

A patient should not need to:

- Search for doctors on one platform.
- Search for nurses somewhere else.
- Call multiple pharmacies to find medicine.
- Search separately for laboratories.
- Use another service to look for blood donors.
- Manage appointments through phone calls.
- Keep healthcare-related information scattered across different places.

Instead, TechCare brings these activities into one connected platform.

3. The Problem
3.1 General Healthcare Access Problem

Patients may face several difficulties when attempting to access healthcare services.

These difficulties can include:

Finding an appropriate doctor.
Finding a provider near their location.
Comparing healthcare providers.
Knowing the available service and its price.
Finding providers who offer home services.
Finding a nurse for a home visit.
Finding a pharmacy that has a required medicine.
Finding nearby laboratory services.
Coordinating blood donation needs.
Managing multiple appointments.
Keeping healthcare information organized.
Communicating with providers through disconnected channels.

These problems can become more difficult for:

Elderly people.
People with mobility limitations.
Patients who require home healthcare.
Patients who have difficulty traveling frequently.
People who need multiple healthcare services.
